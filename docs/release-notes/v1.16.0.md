## Kill Clipper v1.16.0

### Highlight reel cut to your music
- Pick any song (mp3, wav, ogg, m4a, flac) and the reel finds its tempo and bar lines right away. The screen shows the BPM and how fast it will cut.
- The cut pace follows the song: fast music (140 BPM and up) cuts every half bar with short run-ups, slow music (under 100 BPM) lets each clip play for whole bars. Every cut and every kill lands on the beat.
- Fast songs such as drum and bass, phonk and hardstyle are read at their real speed instead of half of it.
- With your own song and **How many: Auto**, the reel uses every different fight, best first, until the song is full (up to 3 minutes). The same fight is never used twice, and streaks are spread through the reel.

### No more loading screens in the reel
- Clips now remember when a loading or connecting screen showed, or when the server moved you to another world. The reel never cuts across those moments, so the lobby after a win and the arena loading in before a duel stay out.
- Older clips and the instant replays are checked by picture: nearly black or blurry loading frames are left out.

Download **killclipper-1.16.0.jar** below and put it in your `mods` folder in place of the old one. Only that one file is needed.
