# Kill Clipper v1.10.1

## Clearer exports
- The editor's saved settings (`.kcedit` files) no longer sit next to your clips, where they looked like the exported video. They now live in a hidden `.edits` folder; old ones are moved there automatically.
- Your exported video is the MP4 / MOV / MKV in `killclips/Edited`. Pick the type with the button next to Export.
- Export now tells you right away when recording is off, instead of playing the whole clip first.
- Pressing Esc during an export stops it and takes you back to the editor with a message, instead of recording the pause menu into the video. Closing the replay mid-export also says the export was stopped.
